Hi, I’m Kit. I’ve spent the last 16+ years working on data platforms, cloud systems, and software at some of the world’s biggest companies. Along the way I’ve helped teams turn complex data problems into real solutions, built roadmaps that actually get used, and worked closely with leaders from the C-suite to engineers in the trenches. I love bridging the gap between strategy and hands-on execution, mentoring others, and finding practical ways to make data work for people.

Outside of work, I’m active in the St. Louis community: I organize the [STL Big Data I.D.E.A. meetup](https://www.meetup.com/St-Louis-Big-Data-IDEA/), teach kids how to code with [1904Coders](https://1904labs.com/about/community/1904coders/), and facilitate a [NAMI Family Support Group](https://www.namistl.org/).
